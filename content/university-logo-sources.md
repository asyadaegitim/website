# University logo normalization — 2026-11-05

The legacy university PNG set contained a number of *reverse/white* marks intended for dark backgrounds. On our white cards these became invisible. Rather than paint black boxes behind them, the site uses high-contrast navy presentation filters for those reverse-only assets, while retaining the underlying logo shapes. Filters are applied only to a precise allowlist. All logo-bearing panels have white backgrounds and `object-fit: contain`.

Xi’an Jiaotong University's old white-on-maroon banner has been superseded by a blue-on-white institutional logo (`xian-jiaotong-university-white.webp`) reproduced from a university partner publication: https://www.samdu.uz/en/announcements/50431 (source image: https://www.samdu.uz/upload/cover-images/6577de6eee13f-6577de6eee140-6577de6eee141-6577de6eee142.jpg). Optimized locally without cropping.

Check the university's current brand guidelines before reusing the institutional image for other designs. University marks are used solely to identify institutions and do not imply endorsement.
